How It Works

Device Testing

Our tool creates a simulated call environment to test how your device handles multiple incoming calls. This helps identify potential issues with call handling, battery drain, and thermal performance.

Network Evaluation

The simulation provides insights into your network's reliability during high traffic conditions. Monitor call success rates to evaluate your carrier's performance under stress.

Legal & Safe

This is a local simulation only. No actual calls are made to the number you provide. The tool is designed for legitimate testing purposes with no harmful effects.
